The Economics of Constitutional Compliance

From February 2018 to January 2023, a joint research project of the Universities of Hamburg and Warsaw investigated the causes and consequences of the differing binding force of national constitutions.

Since 2024, our team has been working on a follow-up project on constitution-making, also funded by DFG and NCN.
