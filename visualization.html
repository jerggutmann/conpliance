The Economics of Constitutional Compliance

Data Visualization

Explore the indicators of the Comparative Constitutional Compliance Database (CCCD) on a world map or as time trends. The charts show today's states only. Variable names are those of the dataset. Hover over the chart for values. You can download the full dataset.

Data: CCCD version 2.0 (1900–2020)

Citation

Please cite the following article when using any visualizations in publications:

Gutmann, J., Metelska-Szaniawska, K., & Voigt, S. (2024). The comparative constitutional compliance database. The Review of International Organizations, 19(1), 95–115. DOI
