The Economics of Constitutional Compliance

Data

Comparative Constitutional Compliance Database (CCCD)

The CCCD offers indicators of country-level compliance with national constitutions in 175 countries since 1900. Updates are published in the repository about once per year. Version 3, based on a wider set of indicators, will be released in early 2027.

Download from Harvard Dataverse

Citation

Please cite the following article when using any version of the database:

Gutmann, J., Metelska-Szaniawska, K., & Voigt, S. (2024). The comparative constitutional compliance database. The Review of International Organizations, 19(1), 95–115. DOI
